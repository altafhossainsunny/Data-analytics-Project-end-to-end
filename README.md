# Banking Data Analysis

This project explores customer demographics and financial behavior using a banking dataset of ~3,000 records. The analysis combines **categorical insights** (gender, nationality, loyalty, income bands) with **numerical trends** (deposits, loans, balances, properties, risk weighting).

## 📊 Key Features
- **Demographics**: Balanced gender split, European & Asian dominance, majority of customers aged 40+.
- **Income & Fees**: Most customers fall into medium income bands and high fee structures.
- **Loyalty Programs**: Jade class is the most common, followed by Silver; Gold and Platinum are rare.
- **Financial Behavior**:
  - Majority hold one credit card.
  - Deposits and savings are modest for most, with a few high-value outliers.
  - Loans and business lending show right-skewed distributions.
  - Property ownership is concentrated at 1–2 properties.

## 📈 Dashboards
- **Categorical Analysis**: Bar and pie charts for gender, nationality, loyalty, income, and credit cards.
- **Numerical Trends**: Histograms + KDE plots with box plots for deposits, loans, balances, accounts, and risk.
- **Trend Analysis**: Line charts for risk over time, funnel charts for loyalty income, scatter plots for loans vs properties.

## 🔑 Insights
- Core customer profile: Medium income, high fee, Jade loyalty, one credit card.
- Growth opportunity: Converting Jade/Silver customers into Gold/Platinum tiers.
- Regional strategy: Increase presence in USA, Australia, and Africa to diversify customer base.

## 🚀 Tools Used
- **Python**: Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib for preprocessing and visualization.
- **SQL**: Query design and integration for structured data extraction.
- **Power BI**: Interactive dashboards, DAX measures, custom bins for age/income segmentation.
